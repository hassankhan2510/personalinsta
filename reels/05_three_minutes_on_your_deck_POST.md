UNDER 3 MINUTES ON YOUR DECK.

## Caption
Investors spend under 3 minutes on your pitch deck. ⏱️ (DocSend data)

Decks that didn't raise got ~90 seconds. Decks that raised got 4+ minutes.

Make these 3 slides impossible to skip:
1️⃣ Traction: one chart, up and to the right
2️⃣ Team: why you, in one line
3️⃣ Market: how big, with a source, not a guess

If they have to search for your numbers, you've lost them.

Save this before your next pitch.
